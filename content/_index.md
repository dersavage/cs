Welcome to my all-things-computer-science site.

Here I will be adding mainly three kings of projects:

1. Tutorials, animations, slides, or other pedagogically related stuff that I have made either for the public or for my students.

2. My own software projects, the ones I make for myself, in my free time.

3. Essays on various aspects of software development and CS.


I am neither pro- nor anti-AI. I just don't believe it has the potential to replace the human programmer. As a result, I do use AI but responsibly, which means:


a. I guide AI until it achieves what I had in mind.

b. When I feel the need, I jump right in and take over from AI to ensure maximally optimal quality, and accuracy.

c. I use AI only where it's suitable. So, not all the time, not everywhere. Where used, I clearly indicate.

d. How much AI? This is a tough call. The process is so intertwined that it's hard to get a meaningful and certain range of percentage values.

e. I am strictly against SLOP. I use frontier models and user them in ways that amplifies their power. However, my use of AI is only restricted to certain hobby software projects. All natural language writing is done by me, your host.
