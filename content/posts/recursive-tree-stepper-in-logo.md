This is a stepper I made for my students but it might benefit others who pass by here as well.

Often, recursion is explained in terms of a factorial example, which is the worst example for it.

Other times, induction is discussion but without reference to the computer science aspect of things.

I wrote this to fill that gap.

We use Logo, of course, since it's the best language to break into programming with.

The stepper shows exactly what happens at which time. It is designed to help you understand how (simple) recursion really works.
